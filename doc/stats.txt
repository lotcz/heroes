Stats
-----

  Experience
  Level
  Skill points
  Attribute points
  Hunger
  Thirst

Attributes:

Improved for attribute points.

  Strength (health and melee damage)
  Dexterity (evasion and ranged accuracy)
  Wisdom (spell power)

Skills:

Learned and improved with teachers for skill points.

  Melee weapons (melee accuracy)
  Ranged weapons (ranged accuracy)
  Fire magic
  Water magic
  Nature magic
  Blood magic

Properties:

Calculated from skills and items.

  Health (10 + level * strength)
  Movement (main protagonist level/10)
  Melee accuracy (melee weapons)
  Melee damage (weapon + strength)
  Ranged accuracy (ranged weapons + dexterity)
  Ranged damage (weapon + arrows)
  Armor (equipped armor)
  Evasion (dexterity)
  Fire resistance (Fire magic + wisdom)
  Poison resistance (Blood magic + wisdom)

Effects
-------

Artifacts can provide permanent effects when worn. Consumables can do immediate effects or cause temporary effects.

Technical

Effect source (artifact, consumable) hold list of effects permanently.
When artifact is worn, inventory controller will insert effect to UnitStatsModel.effects.
When consumable with temporary effect is consumed, it will be permanently inserted into UnitStatsModel.temporaryEffects.
UnitStatsController will insert each temporary effect into UnitStatsModel.effects and remove it when expired.
UnitStatsController will distribute effects to respective StatModel.
StatModel has effects associated to it ephemerally, they increase or decrease effective value.

Fighting
--------

Attacks cause physical and optionally also fire or poison damage.

Melee Attack:
 hit chance: Melee weapons
 damage: equipped weapon + Strength

Ranged Attack:
 hit chance: Ranged weapons + Dexterity
 damage: equipped weapon

Evade: Evasion
Resist Physical damage: Armor
Resist Fire damage: Fire resistance
Resist Poison damage: Poison resistance

Magic
-----

Spells can be learned from teachers when condition is met.
Requirement is always magic school level.
All spells can only be used when camping and usually result in producing an item.

Fire magic

Allows to make fire and later even some flaming weapons.

1  Build fire (wood)
2
3  Torch (wood + pitch)
5
5  Fire arrows (wood + pitch + stone)
8  Fire breath (oil)
10 Fire bomb (oil + pitch + pot)

Water magic

Allows finding water, cooking and making of magic potions.

1  Find water
2  Fishing
3  Cook meat
4  Cook soups
5  Rafting
8  Brew wine & beer
10 Brew potions

Nature magic

Allows finding food, hunting and later creating magic talismans affecting animals.

1  Find berries
2  Find mushrooms
3
5  Hunting
6  Find herbs
8  Wolf talisman (wood + wolf teeth, tame wolves)
10 Lion talisman (rock + lion fur, tame lions)

Blood magic

Allows making poisons and creating blood talismans affecting humans.

1  Poison Spit (temporarily blinds opponents)
2
3  Make poison arrows
5  Cannibalism (cook human flesh)
8  Kurare
10 Blood talisman (rock + human flesh, temporarily calm human enemies)
