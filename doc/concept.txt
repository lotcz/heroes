Heroes

Simplistic RPG, main protagonist must complete main quest. Kobok style low fantasy world

- single party
- one main hero as party leader
- followers: other heroes or other units
- each human can have items, spells
- permadeath
- relations per faction
- can finish city quests to unlock recruit and shopping possibilities
- each turn you either attack, move or rest, then monsters move
- saving only when resting and at water springs


Monsters
--------

Ocean: Kraken/Sharks/Seal/Whale/Lightning storm

River: Piranhas/Crocodile

Beach: Turtle/Giant Crab

Swamp: Baba Jaga/Swamp Sloth/Viper

Forest: Wolf/Warg/Bandit/Eagle/Brown bear

Jungle: Sloth/Tiger/Puma/Anaconda/Gorilla

Desert: Lion/Cobra/Vulture

Mountain: Snow Tiger/White wolf/

Snow: Snow Fox/Polar bear

Tundra: Black bear

Factions
--------

 - race (Yuki/Adelan/Kalinga/Troglodyte)
 - type (Bandits/Tribe/Clan/Priests/Kingdom)
 - diety (Lesan/Vodan/Ohnyan/Siwa/Morana)
 - leader (Chieftain/King/)

Generator
---------

- choose hero
  - Male/Female
  - race
  - faction (type, diety)
  - class (Warrior/Shaman/Hunter)

- race determines start location:
  - Yuki: Snow or mountains
  - Adelan: Grassland or
  - Kalinga: Jungle
  - Troglodyte: Tundra

Regions
-------

- continents
- areas (forests, mountains, deserts, swamps, ...)
- islands
- seas
- oceans
- rivers
- creeks


Story and quests
----------------

- randomly generate story hallmarks, hero's journey
