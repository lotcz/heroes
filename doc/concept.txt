Heroes

Simplistic RPG, main protagonist must complete main quest.

- single party
- one main hero as party leader
- followers: other heroes or other units
- each can have items, spells
- relations per faction
- can finish city quests to unlock recruit and shopping possibilities
- each turn you either attack, move or rest, then monsters move

Monsters
--------

Water: Kraken/Octopus/Water Elemental

Swamp: Baba Jaga/, Kostěj, , čert nebo upír.

Forest: Wolf/Warg/Ogre/vlkodlak/Diva

Factions
--------

 - race (Yuki/Adelan/Rabar/Kalinga/Caerelus/Troglodyte)
 - type (Bandits/Tribe/Clan/Priests/Kingdom)
 - diety (Lesan/Vodan/Ohnyan/Siwa/Morana)
 - leader (Chieftain/King/)

Generator
---------

- choose hero
  - Male/Female
  - race
  - faction (type, diety)
  - class (Knight/Berseker/, Mage/Shaman/Druid, Archer/Rogue/Hunter)

- race determines start location:
  - Beach/Shore (Men)
  - Woods (Adelan/)
  - Mountains (Yuki/Rabar)
  - Underground (Draconian/Orc)
