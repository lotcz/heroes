Heroes

Simplistic RPG, main protagonist must complete main quest. Kobok style low fantasy world

- single party
- one main hero as party leader
- followers: other heroes or other units
- each human can have items, spells
- permadeath
- relations per faction
- can finish faction quests to unlock recruit and shopping possibilities
- each turn you either attack, move or rest, then monsters move
- saving only when resting and at water springs

Factions
--------

 - race (Yuki/Adelan/Kalinga/Troglodyte)
 - type (Bandits/Tribe/Cult/Kingdom)
 - diety (Lesan/Vodan/Ohnyan/Siwa/Morana)
 - leader (Leader/Chieftain/Guru/King)

Generator
---------

- choose hero
  - Male/Female
  - race
  - faction (type, diety)
  - class (Warrior/Shaman/Hunter)

- race determines start location:
  - Yuki: Snow or mountains
  - Adelan: Grassland or
  - Kalinga: Jungle
  - Troglodyte: Tundra
