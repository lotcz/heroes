Story and quests
----------------

- randomly generate story hallmarks
 - Factions: enemy, friendly and neutral
 - Adversary: enemy faction leader or dragon/demon (most of the game you chase this)
 - Real Threat: powerful wizard or god or magical being (you defeat this in the endgame)
 - Mentor: leader of your or friendly faction, or some deus ex machina
 - Other world: another world, deep cave or secluded region
 - Elixir: weapon or some artifact, kidnapped king etc.

depth: number of steps/subquests
level: quest difficulty

Hero's journey
--------------

Departure

- meet your mentor and know your enemy
- smaller version of the whole journey (departure/initiation/return)

Initiation

- travel to other world
- find the elixir

Return

- return with elixir and defeat the adversary
- new enemies and other changes to the old world
- defeat the real threat

Story Templates
---------------

"Adversary"

One of other factions want your faction eradicated.

"Save the King"

Someone kidnapped your leader or stolen an artifact.

"Royal Heir"

You are lost heir to the throne.

"Revenge"

Your family member was murdered and you must revenge them.

Quest types
-----------

Fetch

- fetch specific item

Messenger

- receive an item and bring it to someone else (reverse fetch)

Kill

- learn about the enemy
- kill and return for reward

Find

- river source
- mountain
- NPC
- ritual stone

Rescue

- learn about lost one
- bring him back

Supply

- bring certain quantity of items

Technical
---------

Quests are in a table, each has ID. Quests can be linked to parent quest that affects display in journal.

NPCs can have a single or multiple quests to start or finish. When hero talks to NPC, quest start and finish condition
are evaluated and if empty or fulfilled, protagonist can start or finish quest through dialog option.

Quests can also be auto-activated or finished when conditions are fulfilled.

When quest is started, it appears in journal. When quest is finished, reward is given to party.

QuestModel
- quest id
- parent quest id
- activation conditions (QuestConditionsModel)
- activation cost (QuestCostModel)
- activation reward (QuestRewardModel)
- auto-activate (BoolValue)
- is active (BoolValue)
- is visible in journal (BoolValue)
- finish conditions (QuestConditionsModel)
- finish cost (QuestCostModel)
- auto-finish (BoolValue)
- finish reward (QuestRewardModel)

QuestConditionsModel
- collection of QuestConditionModel
- fires when one of those is completed

QuestConditionModel
- fires when all below are fulfilled
- finished quests (list of quest IDs that must be finished)
- has specific artifact in inventory
- has number of items of certain type in inventory
- has killed specific monster or NPC
- protagonist is above certain level

QuestRewardModel
- artifact id
- number of items of certain type
- experience
- faction reputation (can also be negative)
- can recruit NPC after rewarded
- unlock learning from NPC
- activates a quest

QuestCostModel
- these are evaluated together with conditions, but are spent on start or finish
- artifact id
- number of items of certain type

Example 1 - supply quest
------------------------

quest #1, activates through dialog, "Bring 10 pelts to Carl", finishes through dialog when 10 pelts are in the inventory

Example 2 - messenger quest
---------------------------

quest #1, activates through dialog with Ronan, "Take letter from Ronan to Sandra", force activation of quest #2
quest #2, "Take letter to Sandra", parent is quest #1, finishes through dialog with Sandra letter is in the inventory, force activation of quest #3
quest #3, "Return to Ronan", parent is quest #1, finishes through dialog with Ronan when quest #2 is finished, force finish of quest #1

Example 3 - kill quest
----------------------

- party visits NPC named Steve in Ishar
- Steve has a quest "Kill bandit John in Arborea" (quest id: 1 is in his "quest activator" list, quest has no stat condition)
- Dialog says "I have a quest for you, I need someone killed"
- protagonist selects "Accept quest to kill bandit John"
- this activates quest which appear in journal as "Kill bandit John in Arborea - Steve from Ishar wants you to kill bandit John in Arborea"
- Arborea is added to "heard about" regions, party can now ask "Where is Arborea?" and may receive directions
- when party arrives to Arborea, it is revealed on map, added to discovered locations and disappears from dialogs
- while in Arborea, party can ask in dialogs "Where is John?" and may receive directions
- when party kills John, it disappears from dialogs
- quest in journal now says "Kill bandit John in Arborea - return to Steve in Ishar"
- when in Ishar, party can ask "Where is Steve?"
- when talking to Steve, party selects option "John is dead"
- quest is finished and removed from journal, party receives reward

quest #1, activates through dialog, "Kill bandit John in Arborea", force activation of quest #2
quest #2, "Steve from Ishar wants you to kill bandit John in Arborea", parent is quest #1, auto-finishes when John is dead, force activation of quest #3
quest #3, "Return to Steve", parent is quest #1, finishes through dialog with Steve when quest #2 is finished, force finish of quest #1

Chaining
--------

How to chain multiple quests?

Serialize

- quests follow each other from the same quest giver or multiple
- final reward is given after last in chain

Encapsulate

- insert subquest inside another one
